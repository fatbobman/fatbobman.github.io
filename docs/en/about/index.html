Hi, I'm Xu Yang, aka Fatbobman

A chance opportunity led me to become an Apple ecosystem developer. Initially, my goal was to use this as a means to rehabilitate my body (specifically to improve brain function dulled due to medication trauma). Unexpectedly, as I invested more and more time, it has now become a significant part of my life.

iOS DeveloperBloggerAI Explorer

About This Blog

The primary purpose of creating this blog was to document the challenges I encountered in my learning journey. As my readership has grown, I've also started to consciously write articles that are more suitable for a wider audience. Over time, this ongoing writing and Swift Weekly curation were also featured in Apple's Developer Community Recognition. I see it less as a badge and more as a reminder to keep explaining real problems clearly and sharing useful work with the community.

Current Focus

Currently, I am mainly focused on technologies like Swift, SwiftUI, Core Data, and SwiftData. As my knowledge continues to expand, I look forward to exploring more technical areas in the future.

Over the past two years, AI has become part of how I build and write. Rather than chasing buzzwords, I'm more interested in what these tools can and can't do in real work, and why they behave the way they do. I'll keep sharing that hands-on experience and understanding here.
